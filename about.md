I am an infrastructure and platform engineer in Upstate New York, with 20+ years running production systems end to end.

For eight years I was the sole IT owner of the [Weill Institute for Cell and Molecular Biology][1] at Cornell University, supporting research in structural genomics and simulation, cryogenic electron microscopy, and computational morphodynamics. More recently I handled Tier 2 escalation across Windows, macOS, and Linux for a regional managed service provider.

Today I design and operate a heterogeneous 20-host platform running 110+ services behind SSO, a CrowdSec IDS edge, self-hosted GitLab CI, and Prometheus observability.
